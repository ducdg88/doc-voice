Maya found the letter under the floorboards of the kitchen in her grandmother house. The paper was yellow and soft, and the ink had faded to the color of tea. She read it slowly, twice, then a third time. It was written in 1952 by a young sailor to the woman he loved. He promised to return before the spring. He never did.

Maya sat on the cold floor for a long while. She had always believed her grandmother was a quiet, ordinary woman. Now she understood that quiet people often carry the loudest stories. She folded the letter, put it in her pocket, and walked to the harbor. The sea was gray and calm. She stood there until the sun went down, and for the first time she felt close to a person she had never really met.

When she finally went home, she made a cup of tea, opened her laptop, and began to write down everything she remembered about her grandmother. It was a small start, but it felt important.
