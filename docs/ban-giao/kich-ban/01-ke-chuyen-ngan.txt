The old lighthouse stood at the edge of the cliff for over a hundred years. Every night, its keeper climbed the narrow stairs and lit the lamp. Ships passed in the dark and never knew his name. One stormy evening, a small boat lost its way. The keeper saw it, and he did not hesitate. He rang the great bell until his hands were sore. By morning, the boat was safe in the harbor. Nobody thanked him. Nobody had to. He simply went back upstairs and waited for the next night.
