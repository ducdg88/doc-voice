It was dark. The door opened. Nobody was there. Tom stepped in. The floor creaked. He stopped. Something moved. A shadow. Then a sound. Soft. Close. He turned. Nothing. He breathed out. Then he laughed. It was only the wind. He closed the door. Locked it. Went upstairs. Slept badly. In the morning, the window was open. Wide open. He never opened it. Never. Tom stood very still. Then he saw the footprints. Wet. Small. Leading inside.
