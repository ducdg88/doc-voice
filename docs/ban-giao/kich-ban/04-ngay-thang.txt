The story begins on March 3, 1847, when a ship named the Erebus left the port of Greenhithe. It carried 129 men. On May 19, 1845, the expedition had already sailed north, and by July 28, 1845, whalers saw the two ships for the last time in Baffin Bay. After that, silence.

Search parties went out in 1848, 1850, and again in 1854. In 1859, a note was found on King William Island. It was dated April 25, 1848, and it said that the ships had been trapped in ice since September 12, 1846. The captain had died on June 11, 1847.

It took until September 2, 2014, for a modern team to locate the wreck of the Erebus. Two years later, on September 3, 2016, divers found the Terror. Today, in the 2020s, researchers still pull artifacts from the cold water, and each one adds a line to a story that is almost two hundred years old.
