"Where are you going?" asked Lena.

"To the market," said Jack. "We are out of bread."

"It is almost midnight, Jack. Nothing is open."

"Then I will walk until something is." He smiled, but she did not smile back.

"You are not telling me the truth," she said quietly. "You never go out at night."

Jack looked at his shoes. "All right. I am going to see my brother. He called me an hour ago. He said he needs help, and he said not to tell you."

"Why not?"

"Because he is in trouble again. Real trouble this time."

Lena took her coat from the hook. "Then we are going together," she said. "And do not argue with me. Whatever it is, we will face it as a family."

Jack opened his mouth, closed it, and nodded. They stepped out into the cold night, and the door shut behind them with a soft click.
