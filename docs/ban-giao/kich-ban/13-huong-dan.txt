Making great coffee at home is easier than you think. Here are the steps.

Step one. Start with fresh beans. Buy a small bag, and use it within two weeks. Step two. Grind the beans just before brewing. For a pour over, aim for the texture of coarse sand. Step three. Heat your water to about 93 degrees Celsius, which is just below boiling.

Step four. Place a paper filter in the dripper and rinse it with hot water. This removes the paper taste and warms the cup. Step five. Add 20 grams of coffee and pour 60 milliliters of water slowly in a circle. Wait 30 seconds for the coffee to bloom.

Step six. Pour the rest of the water, about 300 milliliters, in slow circles. The whole brew should take around three minutes. If it is faster, grind finer. If it is slower, grind coarser.

Step seven. Taste it. Adjust. Write down what you did, so you can repeat the good cups. That is the secret, honestly. Small notes, small changes, better coffee.
