Let us look at the numbers. In 2023, the city recorded 1,284,560 visitors. That is up 12.5 percent from the year before, and nearly double the figure from 2015. The average visitor stayed 3.4 nights and spent about $186 per day. Hotels reported an occupancy rate of 78 percent in July, but only 41 percent in February.

Revenue from tourism reached $2.3 billion, which is roughly 18 percent of the whole local economy. The city employs 24,000 people in this sector, and another 9,500 work part time. A new airport terminal, planned for 2027, will cost $640 million and should add 3 million passengers a year.

Not everything is growing, though. The number of small family-owned guesthouses fell from 412 to 297 in just six years. Rent for a one-bedroom apartment rose 34 percent. Locals say the center feels crowded, and some streets see more than 15,000 people on a single Saturday.
