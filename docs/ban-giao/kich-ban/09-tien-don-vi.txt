A standard marathon is 26.2 miles, or 42.195 kilometers. The world record is 2 hours and 35 seconds, set at an average pace of about 21 km/h. A top runner burns roughly 2,600 calories during the race and loses nearly 3 kg of water through sweat.

Now compare that with a daily commuter. A person who bikes 12 km each way travels 24 km a day, or about 120 km a week, and saves around $35 per week in fuel. Over a year, that adds up to $1,820 and prevents about 520 kg of carbon dioxide.

The numbers are even stronger for buildings. Replacing old windows can cut heating bills by 15 to 25 percent. A 5.5 kW solar system costs about $14,000 before incentives and can produce 8,000 kWh a year. If power costs 18 cents per kWh, the payback is roughly 9.7 years.
