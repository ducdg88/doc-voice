Dr. Alvarez joined NASA in 2009 after finishing her Ph.D. at M.I.T. She later moved to the U.S. Geological Survey, where Mr. Chen, the CEO of a small startup, asked for her help with a GPS project. Together they worked with the FBI, the UN, and the WHO on a data system that tracked weather, e.g. storms and floods, in real time.

The team had to explain AI, API, and CPU limits to people who had never heard these words. Prof. Okafor from the U.K. joined by video at 8 a.m., and Ms. Rivera from St. Louis called in at 9 p.m. Their notes were stored on a USB drive, in PDF format, and shared by email, etc.

It was not easy, but by Dec. 12 the demo was ready, and the whole thing ran on a single laptop.
